## Impact

The key `sg.vantagepoint.a.a.a` uses is a compile-time constant, not something derived per-install, per-user or per-session. Anyone who obtains the APK -- which is to say anyone who installs the app, since that is how APKs are obtained -- can read the same key straight out of the decompiled source, with no device exploit required. Observed key material: `8d12…73cc (16 bytes)`.

Practical effect: whatever this key protects (data at rest, a token, an API payload) is protected only against someone who has not looked at the app. It cannot be revoked or rotated without shipping a new build, and it is identical across every install, so compromising one install's key compromises all of them.

## Suggested remediation

Do not embed cryptographic key material as a source constant. Generate or derive the key at runtime and store it in the Android Keystore (`KeyStore` with a `KeyGenParameterSpec`), which keeps it out of the APK and out of memory dumps where practical.
